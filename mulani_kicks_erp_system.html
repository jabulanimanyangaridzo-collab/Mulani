POS offline architecture
Each POS terminal runs a local SQLite database with a service worker. Sales are queued locally when internet is unavailable, then auto-synced to the server when connectivity resumes. Conflict resolution uses server-authoritative timestamps.
Multi-currency: USD + ZiG
All transactions stored in USD (functional currency). ZiG amounts converted at daily RBZ rate (fetched via API). Reports available in both currencies. EcoCash, bank swipe, cash all tracked separately by tender type.
ERP modules
Nine integrated modules — each fully standalone but connected via event bus

Track every SKU by size, color, brand, branch, and supplier. Serial/barcode scanning. Auto-reorder triggers. GRN workflow. Warehouse-to-branch transfers. Stock adjustments with approval workflow. FIFO/AVCO costing methods.

AI & machine learning features
Practical AI built for a sneaker retailer in Zimbabwe — no over-engineering
Model: LSTM
Demand forecasting
Time-series model trained on 12+ months of sales history. Predicts demand by SKU, size, color, and season. Incorporates school term dates, paydays (end-of-month), public holidays. Adjusted for Zimbabwe macro conditions. Output: suggested order quantities per supplier, per product.
Phase 3
Model: Isolation Forest
Theft & anomaly detection
Detects unusual patterns: cash shortfalls at cash-up, voided transactions above threshold, stock variances at branch level, unusual discounts by employee. Flags for manager/director review in real time. Reduces shrinkage and internal theft systematically.
Phase 2
Model: ARIMA + Prophet
Cash flow forecasting
Predicts cash position 30–90 days out. Accounts for supplier payment schedules, rent dates, payroll. Warns when projected cash falls below minimum operating level. Helps director make informed decisions on when to restock vs hold cash.
Phase 3
Social signals
Trend-based buying recommendations
Monitors TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook for trending sneaker models in Zimbabwe and Southern Africa. Correlates social signal spikes with local demand. Surfaces "buy now" recommendations to the director before competitors react. First-mover advantage built in.
